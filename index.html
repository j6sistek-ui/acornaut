THE ROAD TO LAUNCH

Play today.
More ways tomorrow.

Acornaut’s release is pending. The browser preview is playable now; mobile and desktop releases are next.

Where will Acornaut be available?+

We’re working toward releases on the Apple App Store, Google Play, Steam, and Windows. Those releases are planned, not available yet. You can play the current browser preview now.

Will it be free to play?+

Yes. Acornaut is being developed as a free-to-play game with optional in-app purchases. The browser preview is free to try today. In-app purchase details will be shared as launch approaches.

When is launch?+

Release dates haven’t been announced. Follow development on Discord for updates as the mobile and desktop versions take shape.

Will my preview progress carry over?+

The browser preview currently saves progress in the browser and device you use. Carryover to store releases and cross-device saves haven’t been confirmed. Clearing browser data can remove your preview save.

What can I try right now?+

Explore the current browser build, try the flight modes, and take on the Star Chart. It’s an evolving preview: features, balance, and progression may change before the mobile and desktop launches.
