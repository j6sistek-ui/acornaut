Before you ask

What it costs, what it isn't, who made it.

What does it cost?

Nothing to play. There is a shop, and it is built to be easy to ignore: the shelf restocks each day, anything you already own leaves it for good instead of being dangled at you, and one featured pack sits at half price. Everything on that shelf is a suit, a helmet, a trail or a pal.

Do I need an account?

No. No sign-in, no email, no download. Your progress lives in the browser you play it in.

Who made it?

One person. Every suit, planet and animation frame in it was painted for this game and nothing else.
